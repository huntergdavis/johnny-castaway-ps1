# Holiday Expansion Design

> 🌐 **Rendered version:** **[/docs/holidays/](https://hunterdavis.com/Johnny-Castaway-PS1/docs/holidays/)** — this doc rendered on the project website with cross-links and prose context. The GitHub copy here is the source.


Historical design ideation for expanding Johnny Castaway PS1 from 4 holidays
to 35 well-distributed, US-centric celebrations. The current art deliverable
does not implement these as full scenes; it reduces each new holiday to one
small transparent 32x32 emblem sprite. See `holidays-pipeline.md` and
`holidays-style-guide.md` for the active art contract.

## January

1. **New Year's Day** — Jan 1
   - Top hat and "2025" sash on Johnny, confetti drifting, champagne bottle stuck in sand.
   - Gold, black, silver.

2. **MLK Jr. Day** — 3rd Monday of January
   - Johnny stands at attention beside a small podium with a paper banner reading "I HAVE A DREAM."
   - Maroon, cream, gold.

3. **Elvis's Birthday** — Jan 8
   - Johnny in pompadour wig and rhinestone collar, tiny guitar leaned against the palm.
   - White, gold, hot pink.

## February

4. **Groundhog Day** — Feb 2
   - A groundhog pops out of a sand-burrow casting a long shadow; Johnny squints at it skeptically.
   - Brown, dawn-orange, gray.

5. **Super Bowl Sunday** — 2nd Sunday of February
   - Johnny in face paint with foam finger; football tee'd up in the sand, tiny goalposts of driftwood.
   - Green, white, brown.

6. **Valentine's Day** — Feb 14
   - Heart carved into the palm trunk, rose in Johnny's teeth, pink heart-shaped clouds.
   - Pink, red, white.

7. **Presidents' Day** — 3rd Monday of February
   - Johnny in tricorn hat saluting a tiny stars-and-stripes; cherry tree sapling next to the palm.
   - Navy, red, parchment.

8. **Mardi Gras** — Tue before Ash Wednesday (movable, ~Feb)
   - Johnny in feathered mask and beads; coconut maracas; jazz notes drifting on the breeze.
   - Purple, green, gold.

## March

9. **Pi Day** — Mar 14
   - A pie cooling on a rock, "pi" symbol drawn in the sand, Johnny in nerd glasses with a slide rule.
   - Cream, brown, chalk-blue.

10. **St. Patrick's Day** — Mar 17 *(existing)*
    - Johnny in green leprechaun hat, shamrocks sprouting around the palm, rainbow over the horizon.
    - Emerald, gold, white.

11. **First Day of Spring** — Mar 20
    - Cherry blossoms blooming on the palm, butterflies, Johnny stretching in the sun.
    - Pink, pale green, sky blue.

## April

12. **April Fool's Day** — Apr 1
    - Whoopee cushion under Johnny's hammock; the sun is wearing a clown nose; palm leaves are upside-down.
    - Yellow, red, blue.

12a. **4/20 Day** — Apr 20
    - Small green leaf and peace-sign medallion.
    - Green, gold, white.

13. **Easter** — movable Sunday (Mar/Apr)
    - Pastel eggs hidden in the sand, bunny ears on Johnny, basket woven from palm fronds.
    - Lavender, mint, pale yellow.

14. **Earth Day** — Apr 22
    - Johnny planting a sapling, a globe-shaped buoy washed ashore, recycling bin made from a barrel.
    - Forest green, ocean blue, earth brown.

## May

15. **Star Wars Day** ("May the 4th") — May 4
    - Johnny in brown Jedi robe wielding a glowing palm-frond lightsaber; tiny droid silhouette in the surf.
    - Beige, deep blue, lightsaber green.

16. **Cinco de Mayo** — May 5
    - Sombrero on Johnny, papel picado bunting between palms, maraca-coconut in hand.
    - Red, green, sunflower yellow.

17. **Mother's Day** — 2nd Sunday of May
    - Bouquet of tropical flowers in a coconut vase; "MOM" drawn large in the sand with shells.
    - Soft pink, lilac, sage.

18. **Memorial Day** — last Monday of May
    - Small American flag at half-staff in the sand; Johnny removes his hat in salute.
    - Navy, red, white.

## June

19. **Father's Day** — 3rd Sunday of June
    - "#1 DAD" sandcastle, Johnny in a "World's Best Castaway" tie, tiny grill smoking on a rock.
    - Steel blue, charcoal, warm tan.

20. **First Day of Summer** — Jun 21
    - Sun is huge and beaming; Johnny in shades, beach umbrella stuck in the sand, popsicle in hand.
    - Aqua, sun-yellow, white.

21. **Pride Day** — last Sunday of June
    - Rainbow flag fashioned from palm fronds, Johnny with rainbow lei, prismatic surf foam.
    - Full rainbow spectrum.

## July

22. **Independence Day** — Jul 4
    - Fireworks bursting over the ocean, Johnny in Uncle Sam hat, sparkler in hand, flag planted in sand.
    - Red, white, navy blue.

23. **Moon Landing Day** — Jul 20
    - A toy rocket on the beach, footprints leading to the surf, the moon enormous and cratered overhead.
    - Slate gray, deep indigo, pearl white.

## August

24. **National Watermelon Day** — Aug 3
    - Slice of watermelon as Johnny's hat; rinds scattered, seeds being spit at a crab.
    - Watermelon red, rind green, seed black.

25. **Left-Handers Day** — Aug 13
    - Johnny waves with his left hand; everything on the island is mirrored — palm leans left, sun on the left.
    - Mint, coral, cream.

26. **Elvis Week / Hawaii Statehood** — Aug 21
    - Hula skirt on Johnny, tiki torches lit, ukulele leaning on the message-bottle.
    - Hibiscus pink, palm green, sunset orange.

## September

27. **Labor Day** — 1st Monday of September
    - Johnny in hardhat and tool-belt; a half-built tiki hut and a "GONE FISHIN'" sign on the palm.
    - Safety yellow, denim blue, work-boot brown.

28. **Talk Like a Pirate Day** — Sep 19
    - Eyepatch and tricorn on Johnny, jolly roger flag from the palm, parrot perched on shoulder.
    - Black, blood red, weathered gold.

29. **First Day of Autumn** — Sep 22
    - Palm leaves tinged orange and red, a few drifting down; Johnny in a flannel shirt over his rags.
    - Burnt orange, ochre, rust.

## October

30. **Columbus / Indigenous Peoples' Day** — 2nd Monday of October
    - A tiny galleon on the horizon; Johnny holds a hand-drawn map and looks confused.
    - Sepia, parchment, navy.

31. **Halloween** — Oct 31 *(existing)*
    - Johnny in witch hat, jack-o'-lantern beside the palm, bats silhouetted against a full moon.
    - Pumpkin orange, black, ghost-white.

## November

32. **Election Day** — 1st Tuesday after 1st Monday of Nov
    - "I VOTED" sticker on Johnny's chest, ballot box made from a crate, tiny donkey/elephant in surf.
    - Red, white, blue (muted).

33. **Veterans Day** — Nov 11
    - Johnny in dress-uniform cap saluting; a wreath of palm leaves leans against the trunk.
    - Olive drab, gold, deep red.

34. **Thanksgiving** — 4th Thursday of November
    - A small cooked turkey on a banana-leaf platter, pilgrim hat on Johnny, cornucopia spilling tropical fruit.
    - Burnt umber, harvest gold, deep red.

## December

35. **Christmas** — Dec 25 *(existing)*
    - Palm decorated as a Christmas tree with shells and starfish, Santa hat on Johnny, stocking on the message-bottle.
    - Christmas red, evergreen, gold.

Total: **36 holidays**, average ~3 per month, no month with zero. Every month has at least one decoration event, so the screensaver always feels seasonally alive.

## Implementation Notes (non-binding)

- **Movable feasts** (Easter, Mardi Gras, Super Bowl) need a small computed-date table; consider a 20-year lookup baked into the ISO since PS1 has no internet.
- **Adjacent holidays** (Christmas/New Year's, Memorial/Father's Day) can share decoration assets where palette overlaps to save VRAM.
- **Multi-day windows**: holidays like Halloween could decorate Oct 28-31 to give players a higher chance of seeing the special art.
- **Layering**: split decorations into (a) Johnny costume, (b) palm/island props, (c) sky/water tint. Each layer can be reused across holidays to keep the asset budget manageable.
