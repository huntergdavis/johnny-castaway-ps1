MISCGAG 1 — Heat melts Johnny

Validated on 2026-05-03 under the FISHING 1 bar.

Pack identifiers

  • ADS dispatch: MISCGAG.ADS scene 1
  • Slug: miscgag1

What this scene is

Johnny gets so hot in the sun that he melts down into a puddle. Confirmed by direct on-PS1 playback observation while capturing the chapter-select thumbnail; the prior “pocket fan, melts” caption-mapping had the melting right but the pocket-fan beat doesn’t appear in the on-PS1 pack.

Validation Notes

High and low tide packs were regenerated through the generic normal/far-left/far-right foreground-only multi-view stitch before visual validation. The signed-off run used the normal high-tide/night route.

Production island placement remains variable-position safe.

Host regression reference: MISCGAG-1 case page — desktop baseline before PS1 validation.

Performance battle card: high tide · low tide — direct rows in the 126-variant matrix (or /perf/ for the full table).
